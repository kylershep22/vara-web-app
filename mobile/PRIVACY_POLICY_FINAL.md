# Privacy Policy for Vara Wellness

**Last Updated:** December 31, 2025

Vara Wellness, Inc. ("Vara," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Vara Wellness mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope & Important Disclaimers

### Not Medical or Healthcare Services

Vara Wellness is a wellness, educational, and self-improvement platform. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical services. Any information, insights, recommendations, or outputs provided through the Services are for informational and educational purposes only and should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or healthcare advice.

### No Emergency Use

The Services are not designed for emergency situations. If you are experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, contact emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional immediately.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

You may provide information directly to us when using the Services, including:

**Account & Profile Information**
- Email address
- Display name or username
- Profile photo or avatar
- Account preferences and settings

**Wellness & Lifestyle Information**
- Goals, habits, routines, and task completion data
- Journal entries, reflections, and notes
- Mood check-ins and self-reported wellness indicators
- Sleep, focus, stress, hydration, or recovery inputs

**Performance & Cognitive Inputs**
- Self-reported focus or productivity tracking
- Participation in challenges or guided activities
- Behavioral patterns derived from voluntary usage

**Community & Social Content**
- Posts, comments, reactions, and group participation
- Messages exchanged with other users

**User-Submitted Media**
- Images, audio recordings, or other files you choose to upload

You are responsible for ensuring that any information you submit is accurate and appropriate.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use the Services, we may automatically collect certain information, such as:
- Device type, operating system, and application version
- Usage activity, feature interactions, and session duration
- Approximate location based on network information (not precise GPS)
- Diagnostic data, error logs, and performance metrics

This information is used to maintain, improve, and secure the Services.

## 3. Sensitive & Health-Related Information

While Vara may collect wellness-related and self-reported lifestyle information, we do not intentionally collect or require:
- Medical records or diagnoses
- Clinical test results
- Biometric identifiers
- Regulated health information subject to healthcare privacy laws

Any sensitive information you choose to share is provided voluntarily and at your own discretion.
