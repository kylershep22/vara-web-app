# Privacy Policy for Vara Wellness

**Last Updated:** December 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to Vara Wellness ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Display name
- Profile information (bio, avatar, interests)
- Account credentials (encrypted password)

### Usage Data

We automatically collect:
- Device information (model, operating system, unique device identifiers)
- App usage analytics (features used, session duration, crashes)
- Log data (IP address, access times, app errors)

### Content You Create

- Goals and habits you set
- Journal entries
- Tasks and plans
- Community posts, comments, and messages
- Uploaded images and media

### Health and Wellness Data

- Mood tracking entries
- Focus session records
- Habit completion data
- Progress metrics

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide Our Services**
   - Create and maintain your account
   - Sync your data across devices
   - Enable social features (connections, groups, messaging)

2. **Improve Our Services**
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve features
   - Identify and fix bugs and crashes
   - Develop new features based on user needs

3. **Personalization**
   - Provide AI-powered wellness recommendations
   - Customize your experience based on preferences
   - Send relevant notifications and reminders

4. **Communication**
   - Send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
   - Respond to support requests
   - Send important service updates (optional marketing communications)

## AI and Data Processing

Our AI features use OpenAI's GPT models to:
- Generate personalized wellness plans
- Provide journal prompts and summaries
- Offer coaching suggestions

When using AI features, your relevant data (goals, habits, recent journal entries) is sent to OpenAI for processing. We do not train custom AI models on your data. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this processing.

## Data Storage and Security

### Where We Store Data

- **Firebase (Google Cloud):** Your account data, content, and usage information
- **Expo:** App crash reports and performance analytics

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Data encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Data encryption at rest
- Firebase Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access
- Regular security audits and updates

## Privacy Settings

You control your privacy through:
- **Profile Visibility:** Choose who can see your profile (Public, Connections Only, Private)
- **Searchability:** Control whether others can find you in search
- **Notification Preferences:** Manage what notifications you receive

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

### Service Providers
- **Firebase/Google Cloud:** Data hosting and authentication
- **Expo:** App distribution and crash reporting
- **OpenAI:** AI-powered features (when you use them)

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to:
- Protect our rights and safety
- Prevent fraud or security threats
- Comply with legal processes

### Business Transfers
If Vara Wellness is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new entity (you'll be notified).

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request account and data deletion
- **Portability:** Export your data in a machine-readable format
- **Opt-Out:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails

### How to Exercise Your Rights

- **Delete Account:** Settings → Account → Delete Account
- **Export Data:** Settings → Privacy → Request Data Export
- **Contact Us:** support@varawellness.com

## Children's Privacy

Vara Wellness is not intended for users under 13 years old (or 16 in Europe). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn we've collected a child's information, we'll delete it promptly.

## Third-Party Links

Our app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies.

## Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we'll send:
- Habit reminders
- Message notifications
- Connection requests
- Group activity updates

You can disable notifications in your device settings or app settings.

## Data Retention

We retain your data:
- **Active Account:** As long as your account is active
- **Deleted Account:** 30 days after deletion (for recovery), then permanently deleted
- **Backups:** May be retained for up to 90 days

Anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

## European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EEA residents have rights under GDPR:
- Right to access, rectify, and erase data
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

Our lawful basis for processing:
- **Contract:** To provide our services
- **Consent:** For optional features and marketing
- **Legitimate Interest:** To improve our services
