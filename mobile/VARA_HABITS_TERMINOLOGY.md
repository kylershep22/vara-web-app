# Vara Habits Terminology Guide
**Date:** January 6, 2026

This document establishes Vara's unique voice for habit formation, inspired by behavior science best practices while maintaining our original brand identity.

---

## Core Concepts (Vara Voice vs. Atomic Habits)

| Atomic Habits Concept | Vara Terminology | Why It Works |
|----------------------|------------------|--------------|
| **Identity-based habits** | **"Who Are You Becoming?"** | Same focus on identity without using "identity-based" term |
| **Votes for identity** | **"Actions Toward"** or **"Steps Taken"** | Avoids "votes" metaphor, uses Vara's journey language |
| **Two-Minute Rule** | **"Start Small"** or **"Quick Start"** | Simple, descriptive, accessible |
| **Never Miss Twice** | **"Bounce Back"** or **"Stay On Track"** | Positive framing, resilience focus |
| **Make it Obvious** | **"Set Your Trigger"** | Action-oriented |
| **Make it Attractive** | **"Find Your Why"** | Purpose-driven |
| **Make it Easy** | **"Start Small"** | Already used above |
| **Make it Satisfying** | **"Celebrate Progress"** | Positive reinforcement |
| **Implementation Intention** | **"Your When/Where Plan"** | Clear, conversational |
| **Habit Stacking** | **"Build On What Works"** | Leverages existing success |
| **Environment Design** | **"Set Yourself Up"** | Empowering, practical |

## Vara's Habit Philosophy

### 1. **"Who Are You Becoming?"**
Instead of asking what users want to achieve, we ask who they want to become.

**Example UI:**
```
"Who are you becoming?"
→ "A person who moves daily"
→ "Someone who writes"
→ "A healthy eater"
```

**Message:**
> "Every action is a step toward the person you're becoming."

---

### 2. **"Quick Start" (Scaling System)**

Vara recognizes three levels of engagement:
- **Full Version:** Your ideal completion
- **Quick Start:** A 2-5 minute simplified version
- **Just Show Up:** The absolute minimum (1-2 minutes)

**Philosophy:**
> "On tough days, showing up is the win. Every version counts."

**Display:**
```
Run 30 minutes 🏃

Having a tough day?
✓ Full version (30 min)
✓ Quick start (10 min)
✓ Just show up (Put on shoes, step outside)

Remember: Tough days build resilience!
```

---

### 3. **"Bounce Back" (Never Miss Twice)**

**Core Message:**
> "Missing once is life. Missing twice starts a new pattern. Bounce back today."

**UI When Missed Yesterday:**
```
⚠️ Bounce Back Alert

You missed yesterday's run.
Don't let it become a pattern.

Even "Just Show Up" counts!

[Complete Today]
```

**Push Notification:**
```
Title: "Bounce Back! ⚡"
Body: "You missed 'Run' yesterday. Any version today keeps you on track!"
```

---

### 4. **"Steps Taken" (Progress Tracking)**

Instead of "votes," we count "steps taken" toward who you're becoming.

**Display:**
```
You're Becoming: A Runner 🏃
Steps Taken: 89
This Week: 6 steps
Current Momentum: 12 days 🔥

Keep going! You're 89% of the way to being a committed runner.
```

**Milestones:**
- 10 steps → "Getting Started"
- 25 steps → "Building Momentum"
- 50 steps → "Committed [Identity]"
- 100 steps → "Established [Identity]"
- 200 steps → "Expert [Identity]"

---

### 5. **"Your When/Where Plan"**

Implementation intentions without the jargon.

**UI:**
```
When will you do this?

⏰ At a specific time → 7:00 AM
🔗 After another habit → After morning coffee
📍 In a specific place → At my desk
💭 When I feel... → When I feel stressed

Preview:
"When I finish my morning coffee, I will run for 30 minutes."
```

---

### 6. **"Set Yourself Up" (Environment Design)**

**UI:**
```
Set Yourself Up for Success

Make it obvious:
[ ] Put running shoes by the door
[ ] Lay out workout clothes the night before

Remove obstacles:
[ ] Delete social media apps (if time-wasting habit)
[ ] Pre-fill water bottle

Create the right context:
[ ] Only write at your desk
[ ] Only meditate in the bedroom corner
```

---

### 7. **"Build On What Works" (Habit Stacking)**

**UI:**
```
You already have a strong "Morning Coffee" routine!
(45-day momentum 🔥)

Want to build on it?

Your plan:
1. ☕ Coffee (automatic)
2. ✍️ Journal for 2 minutes (NEW)

[Yes, build on it] [Choose different time]
```

## Key Messaging Principles

### 1. **Always Positive, Never Shaming**
❌ Don't say: "You failed" or "Streak broken"
✅ Say: "Bounce back today" or "Keep your momentum"

### 2. **Celebrate Every Level**
❌ Don't say: "You only did the quick start version"
✅ Say: "You showed up on a tough day! That's what builds habits."

### 3. **Focus on Process, Not Perfection**
❌ Don't say: "Complete your habit perfectly"
✅ Say: "Every step counts toward who you're becoming"

### 4. **Resilience Over Rigidity**
❌ Don't say: "Never miss a day"
✅ Say: "Life happens. Bounce back is what matters."

### 5. **Journey Over Destination**
❌ Don't say: "Achieve your goal"
✅ Say: "Who are you becoming?"

## Philosophical Foundation

Vara's approach to habits is rooted in:

1. **Self-Compassion:** We celebrate progress, not perfection
2. **Adaptability:** Flexibility is strength, not weakness
3. **Identity:** Focus on who you're becoming, not what you're achieving
4. **Process:** Small steps compound into lasting change
5. **Resilience:** Bouncing back matters more than never falling

**Tagline:**
> "Every step counts. Every day matters. Who are you becoming?"
