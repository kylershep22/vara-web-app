Focus & Time — Blocks and Tasks

First rendered pass at the two unbuilt Focus & Time sections, built as one loop: capture tasks and tag each by demand, then place the heavy ones into a block when your focus rhythm runs strongest. Every screen is written to the north star from the design direction: this helps you put your day down, not do more of it.

All copy is draft for Jen to react to. Icons are placeholders for Vara's rounded 1.5px line set. Tokens and components follow Vara_Mobile_UI_Standards v1.0.

Decisions embodied here (open items marked ? under each screen)

  • Batch axis: energy demand only. Light / Medium / Heavy, the same readiness language as the daily picker. Cognitive-type batching is cut.
  • A task is a name plus a demand tag. Nothing else. No due dates, projects, priorities, subtasks, or reminders. This fence is what keeps Tasks from becoming a to-do app.
  • No calendar grid at MVP. An hour timeline renders empty hours (deficit framing) and, without calendar sync, falsely implies the rest of the day is free. Weekly view is deferred with sync to Phase 2. The open fork is A vs A2: a plain list, or the list plus a three-zone day-shape strip that shows placement without rendering a single empty hour.
  • Focus Rhythms suggests, never places. The suggestion is a pre-filled invitation the user accepts or overrides. Nothing lands on the day without a tap.
  • Deep work is a block type, not a feature. A protected block is just a block marked protected.
  • Up to 3 blocks a day. The cap is framed as sufficiency, never scarcity.
