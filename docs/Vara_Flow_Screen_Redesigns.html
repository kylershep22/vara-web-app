Why box breathing here is the right call

Box breathing (equal in-hold-out-hold) is a regulating, steadying practice that supports calm alertness — a good fit for someone with energy who's about to take on something hard, and it fits genuinely inside 2 minutes. The focus session (10 min floor) stays the right plan for the longer budgets. The budget becomes an engine input that gates which practice you're served:

BudgetWhat it should resolve to
2 minBrief regulating practice (breathing). State picks which: box breathing for revved/ready, extended-exhale for wound-up.
5 minA single short practice that fits the state.
10 minFocus session (10m) or a 10-min practice/routine, by state + situation.
20 minFocus session or a deeper practice.
45+ minFocus session (longer) or a full routine / deep rest.
Two dependencies for Claude Code. This is engine work, not just UI: the plan resolver has to take the budget as an input, and the catalogue needs a short (2–5 min) practice mapped for each state it can land in, or the short branch falls back. The focus timer should keep its 10-minute floor — we branch away from it rather than adding a 2-minute "focus" option that misnames a reset.
